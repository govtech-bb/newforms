Polyclinics

Last updated on October 9th, 2026

Each area in Barbados has a polyclinic that offers health care to the people who live there. This is called a catchment area. Find out which polyclinic looks after your area, when it is open, and what services it offers.

In an emergency

For emergencies, go to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital’s Accident and Emergency Department, which is open 24 hours a day.

Who can use a polyclinic

If you are a Barbadian citizen who needs free health care, there are nine polyclinics across the island.

Find the closest one to you and check its opening hours and services before you go. Call ahead to confirm that the service you need is available.

Cost

Health care at a polyclinic is free.

Polyclinic pharmacies give you prescription medication covered by the Barbados Drug Service free of charge.

What you need

To find your polyclinic you need your home address. You do not need to give any other details.

Find your polyclinic

Check your address against the catchment areas.

This service is being introduced in phases. It currently covers the area served by Eunice Gibson Polyclinic at Warrens and St. Thomas Outpatient Clinic at Rock Hall, which operates as part of the polyclinic.

For information about other areas, contact the Ministry of Health and Wellness at 1 (246) 536-3800 or email info@health.gov.bb.

Services at every polyclinic

Every polyclinic offers:

  • General practice
  • Antenatal and postnatal care
  • Child health and immunization
  • Family planning
  • Dental health
  • Podiatry (chiropody)
  • Nutrition
  • Pharmacy
  • Counselling
  • Physiotherapy and speech
  • School health
  • Community (district) nursing
  • Health promotion
  • Medical records
  • Health programmes for diabetes, high blood pressure, asthma, breastfeeding, men's health and adolescent health.

Specialist clinics

Some polyclinics also offer specialist clinics, such as mental health, dermatology, gynaecology and minor surgery. These clinics run on set days, which vary by location. Some are currently unavailable.

Call the polyclinic before you go to find out if the service you need is available.

Opening hours

Opening hours vary by location. Sir Winston Scott Polyclinic is open 24 hours a day, every day.
