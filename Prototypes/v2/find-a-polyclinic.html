Find a polyclinic

Last updated on October 14th, 2026

Each area of Barbados is looked after by a polyclinic. This is called a catchment area. Your nearest polyclinic may not be the one for your area.

Use this service to find a polyclinic or outpatient clinic, and check its opening hours, phone number and directions.

In an emergency

Go to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital’s Accident and Emergency Department. It is open 24 hours a day.

Who can use a polyclinic

Barbadian citizens can get free health care at a polyclinic.

Cost

Health care at a polyclinic is free. If your prescription medication is covered by the Barbados Drug Service, you can collect it for free at a polyclinic pharmacy. Check if your medication is free or subsidised.

How to find a clinic

You can:

  • search by clinic name or place, for example Redmans or Rock Hall
  • search for a service, for example blood testing or dental health
  • filter by parish
  • use your location to find clinics near you

If you live in the Eunice Gibson Polyclinic area, the search can tell you which polyclinic is yours. For other areas, contact the Ministry of Health and Wellness.

Before you go

Opening hours vary. Call the clinic before you go to check that it is open and offers the service you need.

Sir Winston Scott Polyclinic and the David Thompson Health and Social Services Complex are open 24 hours a day, every day.
