Find a polyclinic

Last updated on October 9th, 2026

Each area in Barbados has a polyclinic that offers health care to the people who live there. This is called a catchment area. Find out which polyclinic looks after your area, when it is open, and what services it offers.

In an emergency

For emergencies, go to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital’s Accident and Emergency Department, which is open 24 hours a day.

Who can use a polyclinic

If you are a Barbadian citizen who needs free health care, there are nine polyclinics across the island. Find the closest one to you and check its opening hours and services online before you go.

While opening hours vary by location, Sir Winston Scott Polyclinic is open 24 hours a day, every day. Call ahead to confirm that the service you need is available.

Find a polyclinic

Cost

Health care at a polyclinic is free. Polyclinic pharmacies provide prescription medication covered by the Barbados Drug Service free of charge.

Get help

Find out which polyclinic is for your area by entering your address or contacting the Ministry of Health and Wellness on (246) 536-3800 or info@health.gov.bb.
