St. Philip Polyclinic

Last updated on October 28th, 2026

Polyclinic

Six Roads Cross Roads, St. Philip

Get directions Call the Ministry

In an emergency

Call 511 for an ambulance, or go to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital’s Accident and Emergency Department. It is open 24 hours a day.

Opening times

Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm. Closed at weekends.

This polyclinic does not offer urgent care. For urgent care at any time, go to Sir Winston Scott Polyclinic or the David Thompson Health and Social Services Complex. On weekday evenings, you can also go to Branford Taitt Polyclinic (until 6:30pm), Maurice Byer Polyclinic (until 8:30pm) or Randal Phillips Polyclinic (until 8:30pm).

Public holidays: polyclinics may close or shorten their hours on public holidays. Call before you go.
