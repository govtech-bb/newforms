Frederick Miller Polyclinic

Last updated on November 12th, 2026

Hours and days can change. Call before you go.

Glebe, St. George

Get directions Call (246) 536-3940

In an emergency

Call 511 for an ambulance, or go to the Accident and Emergency Department (A&E) at the Queen Elizabeth Hospital (QEH). It is open 24 hours a day.

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm.

This polyclinic does not offer urgent care. For urgent care, go to:

  • Winston Scott Polyclinic or the David Thompson Health Complex, at any time
  • Branford Taitt Polyclinic, weekdays until 6:30pm
  • Maurice Byer Polyclinic or Randal Phillips Polyclinic, weekdays until 8:30pm

Urgent care is for when you are sick or hurt and it cannot wait, but your life is not in danger.

Public holidays: The polyclinic is closed, including its pharmacy and medical records.

Services

This polyclinic offers:

  • Seeing a doctor (general practice)
  • Family planning and contraception
  • Dental health
  • Foot care (podiatry)
  • Diet and nutrition advice
  • Counselling
  • Physiotherapy and speech therapy
  • Nurses who visit your home (district nursing)
  • Advice on staying healthy
  • Medical records
  • Diabetes care
  • Blood pressure care
  • Asthma care
  • Breastfeeding support
  • Men’s health
  • Teen health

Some services run only on certain days. They are in the timetable above.

Pharmacy

The pharmacy is open Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm.

At this pharmacy, you pay no fee to have your prescription filled. The medication itself may still cost something. Check if you can get free or subsidised medication.

To check it is open and has your medication, call the polyclinic and ask for the pharmacy.

Contact and help

For general questions, contact the Ministry of Health and Wellness on (246) 536-3800 or info@health.gov.bb. If you are overseas, email rather than call. All times on this page are Barbados time.
