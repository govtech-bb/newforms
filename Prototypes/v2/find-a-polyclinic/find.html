Find a polyclinic search

Last updated on October 14th, 2026

Find polyclinics and outpatient clinics across Barbados. Health care is free. Polyclinics appear first. Outpatient clinics are smaller and offer fewer services. In an emergency, go to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital’s Accident and Emergency Department.

Go straight to:

  • Vaccinations for children
  • Blood tests
  • Sexual health testing
  • Dental health
  • Mental health
