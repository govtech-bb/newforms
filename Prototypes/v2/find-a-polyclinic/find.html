Find a polyclinic search

Last updated on October 26th, 2026

Find polyclinics and outpatient clinics across Barbados. In an emergency, call 511 or go to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital’s Accident and Emergency Department.

Search and filter

For example: Redmans, Rock Hall or blood testing.

    We look up places using an outside mapping service. We do not keep what you type.

    Filter results by

    For example: dental health, child health or pharmacy.
