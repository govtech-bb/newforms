    Is my medication free?

    It depends on which group the medication is in. Each medication page tells you its group.

    • Group A: you might get it free if you are 65 or over, under 16, or need it for one of the conditions on the medication's page.
    • Group B: free at polyclinic pharmacies, but only after the Barbados Drug Service agrees you can have it.
    • Group C: you pay for it, but at a lower price.

    Need help?

    Contact the Drug Service if you have a problem getting your medication. For example, if:

    • your ID is not accepted
    • you are asked to pay more than you expected
    • the pharmacy does not have it

    • Email: management@drugservice.gov.bb
    • Address: 6th Floor, Warrens Tower II, Warrens, St. Michael
